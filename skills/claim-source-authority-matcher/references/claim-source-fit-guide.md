# Claim-source fit guide

Use this guide to define the authority required by a claim. It is a routing aid, not a universal ranking of publishers.

| Claim function | Usually needed | Often supplementary only | Common mismatch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current rule, legal text, policy, price, or product specification | Governing text or responsible record owner, with effective date or version | Commentary that accurately points to the operative artifact | Blog or old summary used as the governing rule |
| Organization's announcement, promise, opinion, or stated intent | Authentic first-party statement | Independent coverage for context | Treating the announcement as proof of delivery or impact |
| Historical event or individual experience | Contemporaneous record, witness account, or participant testimony appropriate to the wording | Later synthesis that preserves attribution | Using one account to prove prevalence or the only interpretation |
| Measured value or bounded empirical result | Data or measurement producer with method, population, period, and definitions | Responsible summary of the same measurement | Repeating a number through an aggregator with no recoverable origin |
| Causal effect, safety, or comparative performance | Design and analysis capable of supporting the inference; independent review when stakes require it | First-party study or benchmark with visible conflicts | Vendor marketing or an uncontrolled observation used as independent proof |
| Consensus, prevalence, or state of knowledge | Competent synthesis of the relevant evidence base and its limitations | Individual primary studies | One paper, expert, or anecdote presented as consensus |
| Software behavior in a named version | Maintained specification, source code, tests, or reproducible behavior for that version | Forum answer or tutorial that points to the artifact | Search snippet or outdated answer used as definitive current behavior |
| Financial status or company-reported metric | Applicable filing, audited statement, or governed investor record | News analysis and company presentation | Promotional copy used for an audited or independent claim |
| Community sentiment or user pain | Transparent sample of user-originated accounts suited to the population claim | Product documentation for context | Official marketing used to establish user sentiment |
| Recommendation or decision | Appropriate evidence for every material premise plus explicit value judgments and constraints | General background | One impressive but role-mismatched source carrying the whole recommendation |

## Questions that resolve ambiguous cases

1. Is the claim about what happened, what someone said, what a rule requires, what a measurement found, or what should be done?
2. Who could know this directly, who owns the operative record, and who can evaluate it independently?
3. Does the source expose the artifact or method that gives it standing?
4. Is the source being asked to attest to its own success, safety, leadership, or compliance?
5. Would the same source be fit if the claim changed from “the company announced X” to “X works better”?
6. Does the claim require one direct record, multiple observations, or a synthesis of a field?
7. What exact replacement role would close the gap without broadening into open-ended research?

When no role can be established from inspected material, keep the result `Unassessable`. When the source is useful but cannot carry the claim alone, use `Supplementary only`; do not discard it or overstate it.
